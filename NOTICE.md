# Independence and research notice

Oasis Lights for Mac is an independent interoperability project. It is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Oasis, Mixtiles, Espressif, or Apple. Product
names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

The repository contains original source code, interoperability notes, and
redacted protocol descriptions. It intentionally excludes vendor application
binaries, extracted vendor source, private account data, OAuth material,
Bluetooth Mesh keys, Matter setup data, device identifiers, and notarization
credentials.

The bridge exposes only ordinary lighting commands that have supporting static
or live evidence. Factory reset, provisioning, deprovisioning, room mutation,
and Matter commissioning are not part of its public control surface.
