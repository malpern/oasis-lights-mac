Mode model

White and color are separate engines.

Local RGB control switches the light to mode CC 01 and follows with C6 plus three color bytes. White temperature switches to CC 02 and uses C5 or the combined D2 command.

What works

Official white presets, brightness, arbitrary Kelvin within the supported range, RGB payload generation, and a custom macOS color picker.

What remains

Incoming color-state decoding, empirical gamut calibration, and the local payloads behind dynamic Ocean and Fireplace effects.

Effects are not presets

Ocean and Fireplace appear as RainMaker models. Their local effect transport is not yet known well enough to present as a stable feature.
