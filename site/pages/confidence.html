07 · Confidence

What we know.
What we don’t.

A reverse-engineering project is only as useful as its uncertainty ledger. Every public claim is separated into observed behavior, static decode, protected corroboration, inference, or open question.

Evidence gradedPrivate data redactedUnknowns preserved

The evidence vocabulary

LabelMeaning
Live validatedRepeated against physical lights with an observable result.
Static decodedRecovered from client code with a clear serializer, call site, and meaning.
Protected corroborationSupported by private account or device state, published only in redacted form.
InferredBest explanation that fits available evidence but lacks direct confirmation.
UnknownNamed gap. It remains a question rather than being rounded up to certainty.
ExcludedUnderstood enough to identify but intentionally unavailable for safety.

Research ledger

Confidence by domain.

Power and addressing

95%

Confirmed

  • Both physical lights respond individually and together.
  • C3 payload shape and group addressing behavior.
  • No reprovisioning required.

Still open

  • Complete decoding of ordinary incoming status messages.
  • Long-duration recovery behavior after radio interruptions.

Brightness and white temperature

88%

Confirmed

  • Six official Kelvin presets.
  • C4, C5, D2, timestamps, and mode selection.
  • Official app’s local-first behavior.

Still open

  • Exact transition curves across all fade durations.
  • Photometric calibration of displayed Kelvin.

RGB and effects

66%

Confirmed

  • RGB mode value and three-byte local color payload.
  • Normalized RainMaker color channels.
  • Ocean and Fireplace model names exist.

Still open

  • Local effect payload and parameters.
  • Device gamut and channel calibration.
  • Authoritative incoming RGB state.

Automations

58%

Confirmed

  • Schedule entry schema and 20-entry room limit.
  • Fixed, sunrise, and sunset time types.
  • Timezone, location, schedule, and missing-data commands.
  • Legacy Light Cycle migrates into ordinary automations.

Still open

  • Safe current-state read and full round trip.
  • Unknown-field preservation across app versions.
  • Conflict semantics across multiple clients.

Next questions

The useful frontier.

  • Decode incoming status so commanded and confirmed state can be separated.
  • Recover the local transport for Ocean, Fireplace, and other dynamic effects.
  • Map scene activation beyond opaque active_scene_ids.
  • Read, diff, write, and verify automations without losing official-app state.
  • Validate more models and firmware revisions before generalizing beyond Ambient 2.
How to contribute

Bring reproducible traces, product and firmware details, and a clear evidence label. Never submit credentials, account tokens, Mesh keys, precise home location, or proprietary application binaries.
