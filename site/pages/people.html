The public trail
behind Oasis.

Oasis is a Mixtiles product line. Public attribution is strongest for product leadership, certification responsibility, one iOS provisioning contribution, and current company-wide R&D leadership—not for a complete firmware team roster.

MixtilesProductiOS provisioningIoT R&D

Company context

Oasis is a Mixtiles venture.

Mixtiles publicly lists Oasis alongside Mixtiles, easyplant, and Around. The Android application is published as package com.mixtiles.oasis, while FCC equipment authorization belongs to Mixtiles Ops USA Inc.

That structure matters when searching: “Oasis” alone produces many unrelated companies, and “Mixtile” without the final s is an unrelated single-board-computer vendor.

The clearest code artifact

Mixtiles customized Espressif provisioning for Apple's accessory flow.

The public Mixtiles fork of esp-idf-provisioning-ios adds retrieval of an ESP Bluetooth peripheral using the stable identifier supplied by Apple's AccessorySetupKit. It avoids a second BLE scan, then hands the device back to the standard secure Espressif provisioning flow.

Attribution boundary

The repository does not name Oasis. Its owner, timing, iOS provisioning purpose, and match to the observed ESP platform make it likely relevant to Oasis or another Mixtiles ESP product—but “likely” is not “confirmed.”

Rosenfeld's public profiles connect the same identity across GitHub, LinkedIn, and X.

Founders and organization

The company story is broader than the light.

Eytan Levit and David Spectre are Mixtiles' founders. Eytan provides useful public product-history commentary and is the direct source for Shay Eyal's Oasis launch attribution. No public evidence found in this review establishes either founder as an Oasis firmware author.

A current Engineering Manager / Head of Engineering, Oasis listing describes ownership spanning frontend, backend, hardware, and firmware. It confirms that Mixtiles treats Oasis as a complete IoT platform. It does not explain team history or identify the existing implementers.

What remains private

The public engineering footprint is small.

The Mixtiles GitHub organization exposes only a few repositories. The Espressif provisioning fork is the strongest Oasis-adjacent artifact. This review found no public Oasis firmware, backend, protocol specification, full mobile application source, or reliable firmware-team roster.
