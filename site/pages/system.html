The product boundary

The validated product identifies as ambient2. It is neither a plain Wi-Fi bulb nor an enabled Matter accessory. The visible “room” is a coordination layer spanning local addresses, cloud groups, and onboard schedule state.

Compatibility

Why the official app still matters.

Oasis has a richer domain model than power and color alone. Rooms determine group addressing. Automations are serialized as complete room schedules. Scenes are represented by opaque identifiers. Cloud state helps multiple clients agree.

The Mac bridge therefore behaves as another careful client: it sends known local lighting commands, never re-provisions the devices, and avoids destructive management operations.

Deliberate exclusion

The decoded factory-reset command is documented as a boundary, not implemented as a control.
